In many organisations, the conversation about artificial intelligence is waiting for the great innovation: the perfect model, the definitive platform or the use case that changes everything. Meanwhile, tools already available could help with specific tasks today.

“AI by extension” is a simple strategy: widen the reach of existing technology by connecting it to real processes and putting it in the hands of more people. It is not a new product category, but a way to keep experimentation from becoming isolated from day-to-day operations.

## Start with the work, not the catalogue

First, look for repetitive tasks, difficult searches, manual classification or answers that depend on finding scattered information. Then choose a focused intervention: summarising internal documents, finding approved knowledge or helping prepare a first draft.

The question is not “where can we put AI?” but “which friction is worth reducing, and how will we know we have succeeded?” Time saved, errors avoided and team satisfaction are more useful signals than the number of demos.

## Scale responsibly

A successful trial does not automatically become a reliable solution. Define who validates outputs, what data can be used, how it is protected and what happens when the tool gets something wrong. Train people and redesign the process instead of adding an interface to a workflow that no longer works.

Governance is not a barrier to scale; it is what makes scaling possible without multiplying the same risks. Sensitive use cases need safeguards and oversight that match their consequences.

## Adoption is innovation too

Scaling technology does not mean giving up on innovation. It means creating the conditions for innovation to deliver value: learn from small use cases, share what works and expand gradually with clear metrics and boundaries.

The advantage may not be getting access to the next model before anyone else. It may be knowing how to integrate today’s capabilities into everyday work with judgement, safety and purpose.
