      <p class="og-label">A PROFILE THAT CROSSES BOUNDARIES</p>
      <h2>From industrial processes<br><em>to the classroom and laboratory.</em></h2>
      <p class="og-lead">My experience connects mechanical and chemical engineering, laboratory practice and teaching. I am interested in understanding how things work, analysing their processes and making that knowledge accessible.</p>
      <p>Engineering provides a structured way to tackle challenges: observe the system, identify variables, analyse causes and weigh alternatives. Chemical training and laboratory experience add attention to detail, experimental method and respect for rigorous data.</p>
      <p>In the classroom, that same perspective becomes a way of teaching mathematics through understanding and application. I see teaching as listening and support: creating a welcoming environment, explaining clearly and helping each person find meaning in what they learn. Science, industry and education are not isolated compartments, but fields that can enrich one another.</p>
      <blockquote>Understanding a process, experimenting and teaching share something essential: observing, asking questions and learning from evidence.</blockquote>
