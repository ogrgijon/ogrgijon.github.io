# A screen can transform how a space is experienced

An exhibition, event or waiting room is more than a place: it is an experience shaped by what we see. Merekai Presenter turns a folder of photographs and videos into a full-screen presentation for events, exhibitions, galleries, classrooms and digital signage.

The idea is simple: your images fill the space, not desktop windows. You control the presentation from a panel, while your files and settings remain on the local machine instead of being sent to a cloud service.

## A presentation without exposing the desktop

When an audience is watching, every interruption is distracting. The player keeps the media centre stage while a control panel lets you organise the order, advance the queue or change the pace without losing control.

That continuity matters in a gallery, presentation or information display: content can accompany the space while the operator remains able to step in when needed.

## Designed for purposeful presentations

- Full-screen playback of images and videos.
- Organisation by folders and files, with simple reordering.
- Live controls, visible queue, playback speed and modes.
- Presets and overlays for titles, labels, images and custom styles.
- Scheduled inserts and notices for promotional content or messages.
- Local operation with settings stored in SQLite.

The overlay designer lets you explore a visual identity with adjustable titles, messages, shapes, images and styles. Scheduled inserts can interleave announcements or clips without rebuilding the main collection.

## For spaces that tell stories with images

Galleries and exhibitions, events, demonstration spaces, education, studios and information displays. The control interface and player run alongside a local server.

## From a media folder to a purposeful experience

The software puts tools in the team's hands; content selection and design remain essential. Merekai Presenter provides a foundation for building a more consistent experience and adapting it to a specific space.
